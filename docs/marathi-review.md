# Marathi review checklist

_Generated by `npm run marathi-review` from `src/i18n/mr.json`. Do not edit by hand._

18 strings in the farmer app still have no Marathi. `docs/frontend-spec.md`
supplies none for these, and CLAUDE.md rule 2 forbids machine-translating them, so
the app currently shows the English meaning in the Marathi build. Every one of them
is visible at `/gallery` under Strings, badged `TODO-mr`.

Send the block below as one WhatsApp message. Replies come back as
`key = मराठी`, and land in `src/i18n/mr.json`.

---

```
*BhavSetu — मराठी हवी आहे*

18 strings need Marathi. The app shows English for these right now.
Reply with the number and the Marathi, e.g.  1 = कारण

Rules: farmer speech, not textbook Marathi. Short. No English words
unless a farmer would actually say them.

 1. Why we say this
    Heading of the sheet that opens when you tap विका / थांबा / सावध.

 2. Tap the verdict to see why
    Tiny text on the verdict chip itself, telling you it can be tapped. 2–3 words.

 3. Arrivals are falling in Nashik
    Reason 1 in that sheet. Plain speech, how a trader would say it.

 4. No rain expected for 10 days
    Reason 2 in that sheet.

 5. Festival demand in 2 weeks
    Reason 3 in that sheet.

 6. Saved data · {time}
    Small grey badge in the header when the data is cached. {time} is a clock time.

 7. Try again
    Button under "भाव मिळाला नाही." 1–2 words.

 8. Trust
    Read aloud for the buyer trust bar. Not shown on screen.

 9. Bid
    Money math on a bid: bid − transport − cess = in hand. Label for the bid.

10. Transport
    Same math, label for the transport deduction.

11. Cess
    Same math, label for the mandi cess deduction.

12. In hand
    Same math, the bold final line. Master prompt calls it "tumhala milel" — confirm the exact form.

13. Received this season
    Label above the big season total on the पैसे screen.

14. Extra earned vs harvest-day price
    Label on the closing demo card: what the farmer earned above the harvest-day price.

15. Ask
    The word under the big mic button. ONE short word, must fit a 72px circle.

16. Listen
    The word under the speaker icon, top right of every screen. ONE short word.

17. Close
    Close button on every sheet. 1 word.

18. Step {current} of {total}
    Progress in the 3-step add-lot flow. {current} and {total} are numbers.

```
